# Style Library

Human overview of the styles. Machine-readable specs with exact palette hex, typography, composition, density limits, and failure modes live in `styles/{CODE}.yaml` — read the YAML of the selected style before writing the final prompt.

## ACD - AI Contrast Drama

Template: `assets/ACD-AI-Contrast-Drama-template.jpg`

## WEC - Watercolor Editorial Concept & Cover

Templates:
- Cover mode: `assets/WEC-Watercolor-Editorial-Cover-template.png`
- Character / Subject mode: `assets/WEC-Watercolor-Character-template.jpg`

Use for article covers, event recaps, playbook covers, calm premium banners, AND watercolor character mascots, author personas, botanical/zoological naturalist illustrations.

Style: elegant loose watercolor on warm white cold-pressed watercolor paper (#FAF7F0). Soft blue, deep navy/indigo (#1E3A5F), cream, and gray washes. Transparent layered washes, subtle pigment granulation, natural bleeding edges, and delicate paint splatters.
- Cover mode: Large clean headline in center band, minimal text, decorative corner motifs (follow `references/cover-spec.md`).
- Character / Subject mode: Central watercolor character or naturalist subject with breathing negative space and expressive handcrafted feel.

For full cover composition, text budget, motif, palette, and signature rules, follow `references/cover-spec.md`.

## EIS - Educational Infographic Sketchnote

Template: `assets/EIS-Educational-Infographic-Sketchnote-template.png`

Use for educational explainers, cheat sheets, technical/business terminology guides, knowledge posters, framework overviews with many concepts, and multi-concept comparisons.

## BGL - Bento Grid Layout

Template: `assets/BGL-Bento-Grid-Layout-template.png`

Use for bento grid layouts, SaaS feature lists, modular dashboards, and professional concept grids.

Style: clean modern modular grid layout with rounded corners on deep charcoal (#1A1A1A) background. Contains 4-6 cards of varying sizes. Drawing elements and text in pearl white (#F8F9FA) with highlighted headers/key statistics in aura gold (#FFD700).

## EIL - Expressive Ink Linework

Template: `assets/EIL-Expressive-Ink-Linework-template.jpg`

Use for character mascots, storytelling narrators, author personas, editorial portraits, botanical/nature metaphors (windswept trees, dandelion seeds), and reflective philosophical essays.

Style: handcrafted black ink drawing on warm ivory/cream textured paper (#F5F2EB). Fine liner (0.1-0.3mm) dip pen aesthetics, dynamic hatching and cross-hatching, energetic gestural contour lines, visible motion strokes. Shading and depth are achieved entirely through line density (no digital gradients or flat color fills). Monochrome black (#1A1A1A) on warm paper.
